Pick how much of the day you want to hand over.

Full board

You show up and ride.

Daily feeding, clean and comfortable stalls, regular turnout for exercise and company, and ongoing health monitoring. Every day, done by people who know your horse.

Half board

Stay in the routine without living in it.

Feeding and turnout are shared between you and our staff. Do as much as you like and lean on us for the rest.

Dry board

Your horse, your way, on a better property.

You handle feeding, grooming and turnout. You get a 12' × 12' stall, paddock access and a safe, well-kept facility to do it in.

Every horse is quoted individually, because no two need the same thing. Text Kristina your horse's routine and she'll put a quote together. Bringing more than one horse? Ask about multi-horse pricing.

Questions owners and parents ask.

Why don't you list prices?

Because a four-year-old's first lead-line lesson and a show horse on full board aren't the same job. Text us and you'll get a real number for your situation.

Do you take complete beginners?

Yes. Riders start as young as four, on a lead line.

Can I stay involved in my horse's care?

Yes. Half board shares feeding and turnout with you, and dry board leaves it all in your hands.

How do I know my horse is safe at night?

A security camera system covers the property 24 hours a day.

What disciplines can I ride here?

The 100' × 200' arena works for dressage, jumping, polo practice and general training.

Do I need my own horse for lessons?

Can I watch my child's lesson?

Yes, from the shaded observation lounge.

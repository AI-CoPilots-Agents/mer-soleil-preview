Mer Soleil means sea and sun.

mer, seasoleil, sun

It's in the logo too: a chestnut horse between the Florida sun and the waves. Here that means bright mornings, regular turnout and horses as relaxed as the pony in the stall below.

Two ways to join us.

Board your horse

  • Full boardWe handle feeding, stalls, turnout and health checks. You show up and ride.
  • Half boardFeeding and turnout are shared with you.
  • Dry boardYou do the daily care; we provide the stall, paddocks and a safe place to do it.

Quoted per horse, with discounts for more than one.

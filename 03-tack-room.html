Three ways to board. One standard of care.

Full board

You show up and ride.

Daily feeding, clean and comfortable stalls, regular turnout for exercise and company, and ongoing health monitoring.

Half board

Shared, on your terms.

Feeding and turnout are split between you and our staff. Stay in the routine without living in it.

Dry board

Your hands on everything.

You manage feeding, grooming and turnout, with a 12' × 12' stall, paddock access and a safe, well-kept facility.

Rates are quoted per horse, because the right tier depends on how much of the daily care you want to keep. Multi-horse discounts available.

Ask what's available

Before you visit.

How much does boarding cost?

Rates are quoted per horse after you tell us your horse's routine, and multiple horses are discounted.

What's the difference between full, half and dry board?

Full board: we handle feeding, stalls, turnout and health monitoring. Half board: feeding and turnout are shared. Dry board: you manage the daily care on our property.

How big are the stalls?

All 19 stalls are 12' × 12', across two barns.

Is the property monitored at night?

Yes. A camera system runs 24 hours a day.

Can I bring my own trainer?

Kristina to answer: outside trainers allowed?

Is there a stall available now?

Kristina to answer: current availability or waitlist.

Room for a full dressage court, and then some.

100' × 200' is about 30 × 61 meters. A standard large dressage court is 20 × 60 meters, so a full test fits with roughly ten meters to spare across. Manicured footing, six large mirrors, and space for jumping, polo practice and training.

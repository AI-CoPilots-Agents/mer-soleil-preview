Answers

Answers to what people search for.

How much does horse boarding cost in Loxahatchee Groves?

At Mer Soleil it's quoted per horse, because full, half and dry board are priced differently and multiple horses get a discount. Text (214) 620-9711 for your number.

What's the difference between full, half and dry board?

Full board covers daily feeding, stalls, turnout and health monitoring. Half board shares feeding and turnout with you. Dry board gives you the stall and paddocks and you do the daily care.

How big are the stalls?

12' × 12'. There are 19 in total: 12 indoor and 7 outdoor, across two barns.

Is there a riding arena?

Yes. 100' × 200', manicured, with six large mirrors. It suits dressage, jumping, polo practice and training. There's also a round pen.

Do you offer riding lessons for kids?

Yes, from age four on a lead line, plus group classes and holiday camps.
